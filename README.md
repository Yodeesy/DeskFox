### 📖 Project Overview

**DeskFox** is a **Desktop Application** designed to bring you a cute desk pet with a unique narrative background.

- Currently only supports **Windows**.

On your desktop, this fox (DeskFox) dressed in light green old clothes will accompany you — not just a simple pet, but an embodiment of a gentle story about time, solitude, and memory.

**Key Features:**

- 🗺️ **Deep Narrative:** Every action and appearance is infused with a unique storytelling atmosphere.
- 🤫 **Quiet Companionship:** Provides calm, gentle company without disrupting your work with excessive interaction.
- 🖥️ **Native Desktop Experience:** Runs as a standalone desktop application.

### 📖 项目简介

**DeskFox** 是一款**桌面应用**，为您带来一只拥有独特故事背景的可爱桌宠。

- 目前仅支持 **Windows** 平台。

在你的桌面上，这只穿着浅绿旧衣的狐狸（DeskFox）将陪伴你。它不仅仅是一个简单的宠物，更是承载着关于时间、孤独与记忆的温柔故事的化身。

**主要特性：**

- 🗺️ **深沉的故事背景：** 每一个动作和形象都融入了独特的故事意境。
- 🤫 **安静的陪伴：** 提供安静、温柔的陪伴，不以过度互动打扰你的工作。
- 🖥️ **桌面原生体验：** 作为独立的桌面应用运行。
